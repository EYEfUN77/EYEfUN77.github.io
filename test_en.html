Desktop Calendar (iCalendar)

📱 We're looking for phone app testers

We are bringing Desktop Calendar to Android phones.
To publish it on Google Play, 12 testers need to keep the app installed for 14 days.
The three steps below take about five minutes. Thank you for your help!

You need: an Android phone and the Google (Gmail) account signed in to the Play Store on that phone
What to do: install the app and keep it for 14 days (opening it now and then helps even more)
Cost & ads: free, no ads · your events are stored only in your own Google account
※ On iPhone, open calendar.eyefun.org/web in Safari and choose 'Add to Home Screen' instead (iPhones don't count toward this test).

These buttons work best on your phone.

1

Join the tester group (Google Group)

Open the tester group
  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Gmail account as the Play Store (profile picture at the top right).
  2. Press Join group.
  3. If a settings window appears, press Join group once more.
  4. When you have joined, close that window or press Back. The Step 2 button will appear at the bottom of this page.

Joining the group is only what makes you eligible for the test. No emails are sent through this group, and only the developer can see the member list.

2

Sign up for the test

Open the test sign-up page
  1. Press the blue Become a tester button.
  2. When you see You are now a tester, you're done.
  3. Close that window or press Back. The Step 3 button will appear at the bottom of this page.

Right after Step 1 the page may say it isn't ready yet. Please refresh after a minute or two.

3

Install from the Play Store

Install from the Play Store
  1. When the Play Store opens Desktop Calendar (iCalendar), press Install.
  2. Open the app and press [Sign in with Google], then choose your usual Google account — your Google Calendar appears right away.
  3. Please keep the app for 14 days. You may remove it after that.

If it says the app can't be downloaded, wait about 10 minutes after Steps 1–2 and try again. If it still fails, clear the Play Store cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache) and retry.

FAQ

Is my information shared with the developer?

Under Google Play policy, testers' email addresses are visible to the developer. They are used only to run the test. Your events are never sent to the developer; they stay in your own Google account. See the privacy policy for details.

Will I see the same events as on my PC?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and your PC and phone share the same Google Calendar. Changes on the phone appear on the PC right away.

Where do I send feedback or bug reports?

Use 'Send feedback to the tester' at the bottom of the app's Play Store page, or email sinmasuri@gmail.com.

How do I leave the test?

Press Leave the test on the Step 2 page and remove the app. We'd be grateful if you could keep it for 14 days for the official release.
