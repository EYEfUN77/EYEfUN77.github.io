Desktop Calendar keeps your Google Calendar on the Windows desktop at all times. It sits behind your other windows so it never gets in the way, and you can view, add and edit events right there.

Download · Install

1. Download

Recommended: install from the Microsoft Store — no warnings, and updates are automatic. You can also download DesktopCalendar-Setup.exe (Windows 10/11, about 90 MB). For Arm64 PCs (Snapdragon etc.) use the Arm64 installer (the Store picks the right one automatically). Earlier versions are in the release history.

2. Install

In the Store, just press Get. If you downloaded the installer and "Windows protected your PC" appears, choose More info → Run anyway.

3. Connect Google

Press Sign in with Google in the calendar to connect your Google account, and your Google Calendar events appear on the desktop. The app follows your Windows language; you can switch between English and Korean in Display settings.

Features

📌 Pinned to the desktop

Always behind other windows, so it never covers your work. Clicking the desktop doesn't bring it to the front, and it stays visible when you press Win+D. Size, position and transparency are remembered, and you can collapse it to just the title bar.

🔄 Two-way Google Calendar sync

Shows your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. Events you add, edit, delete or drag in the calendar appear in Google Calendar on your phone right away. Your PC and phone share the same categories and colors.

🚗 Departure alert

Meeting in Seoul at 1 pm? Pick the place and your starting point, then press Auto. Kakao directions estimates the driving time using the traffic forecast for that day and time, adds a buffer (10-minute steps, up to 1 hour), and tells you when to leave — e.g. "leave at 08:00". At that time your PC shows a "Time to leave" alert with sound and a one-click directions button, and Google Calendar on your phone rings too. You can also type the travel time yourself. (Route estimates currently work within Korea.)

📍 Place search + navigation

Type a place name to search Kakao Map and save the exact road address and coordinates with the event (or just type freely). The 🚗 Directions button opens Kakao Map directions with the destination filled in; long-press to choose Naver Map, TMAP or Google Maps.

🎨 Categories, colors and tags

Pick a category such as work, unit or personal, and a [tag] and color are added to the title automatically. Changing a category color updates existing events, while colors you chose yourself are kept. Make important events sparkle.

🔔 Reminders you won't miss

Timed events get a 10-minute reminder automatically, which also rings in Google Calendar on your phone, and you can add a departure alert for trips. On the PC the alert sound repeats for up to a minute until you press OK, with "Remind in 5 min". Alerts, sound and automatic reminders can each be turned on or off.

📎 Photos and documents

Attach photos and PDF, Excel, HWP or Word files to events — just drag and drop. Attachments are saved in your own Google Drive, so they show up in Google Calendar on your phone too.

⚡ Quick add, repeats and D-day

Type one line like "tomorrow 3pm Team meeting @Room 501" and the date, time and place are filled in. Recurring events with this event / following / all editing, event templates and pinned D-days are supported.

🗂️ All your calendars in one place

Add calendars from other Google accounts (school, work) and see and edit them together (PC app). Subscribe to web calendars (ICS·webcal) such as academic calendars, and Import into my calendar to copy them into your Google Calendar so you can fix changes yourself. Create shared calendars for family or colleagues and delete them right from the calendar.

🕘 Timetable view with drag

Switch week or day view to a timetable. Drag events to change time and date, and drag the bottom edge to change length. On phones, long-press and drag.

🗓️ Holidays, week numbers and weather

Choose Sunday or Monday as the first day of the week, show birthdays from Google Contacts (🎂), semester week numbers, anniversaries and weather. Day, week, month and year views, search, printing and Excel/ics export are included.

📱 Phone and offline

Use the same calendar on your phone at calendar.eyefun.org/app — add it to your home screen and it works like an app. The last events you received stay available without internet.

Google data the app uses

Desktop Calendar accesses the information below only after you sign in with Google and grant permission. The app talks directly to Google's servers, and your Google user data is never sent to or stored on the developer's servers, never sold, and never used for advertising or AI model training.

PermissionUsed for
Email address (openid, email)Show and identify the signed-in account.
Google Calendar (…/auth/calendar)Show your calendars and events; when you request it, create, edit and delete events, change recurring events, create a shared calendar and invite members.
Google Tasks (…/auth/tasks)Show your tasks and, when you request it, add, complete or delete tasks.
Google Drive – files created by the app (…/auth/drive.file)Store and show photos and documents you attach to events in the "바탕화면 달력 첨부사진" and "바탕화면 달력 첨부파일" folders, and a settings file that keeps your phone and PC in sync. The app cannot access Drive files it did not create.
Google Drive – app data folder (…/auth/drive.appdata)Keep app settings (categories, colors, semester weeks, stickers) in the hidden app folder so they follow you to other PCs.
Desktop Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
